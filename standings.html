Qanday hisoblanadi?

Poygada 1–10 o'rindagilar ball oladi: 25–18–15–12–10–8–6–4–2–1. Tez aylanish uchun 1 qo'shimcha ball (fastest lap, agar 10 likka kirgan bo'lsa). Sprint poygada esa 8–1 ball beriladi. Jamoa balli — ikki pilosining yig'indisi.

🥇 1-o'rin = 25 ball 🥈 2-o'rin = 18 ball 🥉 3-o'rin = 15 ball ⚡ Fastest lap = +1 ball 🏎️ Sprint = 8–1 ball
